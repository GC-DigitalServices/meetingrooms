PHASE 6 — iPAD ROOM DISPLAYS
=============================

Goal
----
Build the iPad display UI, the QR-based booking handoff into the
portal, the device pairing flow with scope support (STANDARD,
SECTION, COMPOSITE), and the operational pieces needed to run iPads
as permanently-on display screens. By the end of this phase we can
deploy iPads to pilot rooms — including a composite-room pilot —
and have them behave correctly 24/7.

Duration estimate
-----------------
~2 to 2.5 weeks.


Prerequisites
-------------
- Phases 1 through 5 complete.
- At least two physical iPads for testing, with an MDM solution
  available (Jamf, Intune, or similar).
- Wall mounts and power for at least one standard pilot room and
  one composite pilot room (with a section's-door iPad in at
  least one section, and ideally a main-entrance iPad too so we
  can pilot all three scopes).


Design note — what the iPad does
--------------------------------
The iPad is a display. It shows live status and a QR code. It
never authenticates a user, never accepts typed input, and never
writes to Graph. Bookings happen on the user's phone via the
portal, under their MSAL session.

There is no PIN, no on-screen keyboard, no shared-kiosk session,
and no iPad-specific booking endpoint.


Work items
----------
1. Display route group (app/(display)/)
   - Single page /display.
   - Auth via device token in a header set by the PWA wrapper.
   - On load, reads token → device → room → scope, then renders
     one of three layouts (work items 2 and 3).
   - Subscribes to the right channel(s) per scope.

2. STANDARD and SECTION layouts
   - FREE: full-bleed --status-free, room name in very large
     type, capacity below, prominent QR code, caption "Scan to
     book this room", small next-booking preview at bottom.
   - BUSY: --status-busy, current subject (or "Busy" if
     organiser is non-staff or marked private), organiser name
     (subject to visibility rules), "Until HH:MM", "Available
     from HH:MM". QR remains present but subordinate.
   - SOON (free but a booking starts within 15 min):
     --status-soon, countdown, organiser name (if shown),
     subject (if shown), QR present.
   - Transitions cross-fade 200ms.
   - QR ≥ 200pt square, on a white card in BUSY and SOON states.

3. COMPOSITE layout
   - The screen is divided into per-section cards (2-4 sections),
     each showing the section's name, current status colour,
     current/next booking summary, and "free until / busy until"
     timing.
   - A single prominent QR sits at the top or centre of the
     screen, captioned "Scan to book any section or the whole
     room."
   - Layout adapts to section count: 2 sections = stacked top/
     bottom; 3 = three columns or three rows; 4 = 2x2 grid.
   - rooms.yaml validation caps section count at 4 for now.

4. QR token issuer (backend)
   - GET /api/devices/qr-token — auth'd by device token. Returns
     { token, expiresAt }.
   - Token = HMAC-SHA256 over { deviceId, roomId, scope, iat,
     exp }, key from env (QR_SIGNING_KEY). 5-minute TTL.
   - Rate-limited: 60 mints per device per hour.
   - Key rotation: on the same schedule as the client secret
     (every 12 months); old key valid for 24h grace.
   - Logs: signature prefix only (first 8 chars), never the
     full token.

5. QR rendering and refresh on the iPad
   - The display fetches a fresh token every 90s.
   - On each fetch, regenerate the QR code locally (qrcode.js
     or similar).
   - QR encodes:
     STANDARD/SECTION → /r/{roomId}?from=display&t={token}
     COMPOSITE        → /r/{compositeRoomId}?from=display&t={token}
   - If a fetch fails, keep showing the most recent QR for up
     to 4 minutes (within token TTL). After that, replace with
     "Refreshing — please book from your desk."

6. The /r/[id] landing on the portal
   - Built in app/(portal)/ in phase 5; this phase verifies the
     end-to-end flow.
   - For STANDARD/SECTION ids: booking dialog opens directly
     with the section/standard room and the chosen time.
   - For COMPOSITE ids: a chooser page renders first ("Book
     section A / B / C / the whole Hall"), then dispatches into
     the booking dialog.
   - Permission check happens server-side on submit. Students
     attempting to book a staff-only section after scanning a
     COMPOSITE iPad see a polite rejection on the chooser page —
     that section's button is disabled with a "Not available to
     you" label.

7. Device pairing flow (admin tool)
   - Built in phase 5; this phase adds the scope picker UI:
     "What does this display represent?"
       For a STANDARD room: implicit.
       For a SECTION: the admin picks which section.
       For a COMPOSITE room mount (main entrance): "the whole
         room with section chooser."
   - Generates a 6-digit pairing code (10 min TTL).
   - iPad enrols at /api/devices/enroll, receives a 256-bit
     token (Argon2id-hashed at rest), stores in PWA secure
     storage.
   - Revoke from admin tools at any time → deletes
     Device.tokenHash and disconnects active sockets for that
     device.

8. PWA wrapper
   - The iPad runs the app via single-app mode in MDM.
   - Service worker caches the display shell so brief network
     drops don't blank the screen; shows "Reconnecting…"
     subtle banner with last-known state visible.
   - Screen-lock and sleep disabled via MDM profile, not app
     code. Documented in docs/ipad-provisioning.md.

9. Offline behaviour
   - Socket disconnected >30s → small "Offline — refreshing"
     banner.
   - QR replaced with "Refreshing — please book from your desk"
     after 4 minutes (the most recent QR has expired).
   - On reconnect: snapshot reconciles, QR fetcher resumes,
     banner clears.

10. Physical provisioning checklist
    - Hardware: iPad model, case, wall mount, cabling.
    - Mount label with the fallback URL (e.g.
      rooms.college.ac.uk/r/B12) for users without smartphones
      or with cameras that don't auto-detect QR codes.
    - MDM profile: single-app mode, disable screen lock,
      disable volume, configure Wi-Fi, restrict URLs to our
      domain, allow camera (for the admin-side enrolment QR
      scan).
    - Wi-Fi: confirm staff Wi-Fi reaches the mount location
      — phones need it to load the portal after scanning.
    - Power: PoE injector or nearby socket.
    - A one-page printable setup guide in docs/ipad-setup.md.

11. Telemetry
    - iPad sends a heartbeat every 60s over the socket.
    - Backend tracks Device.lastSeenAt.
    - Background job device-heartbeat-check (every 15 min)
      flags devices silent >2h; emails admins if >6h.
    - Counter qr_token_minted per device per hour.
    - Counter booking_via_qr per room per day.
    - Admin dashboard tile: "Displays" with live status per
      device, including scope.


Testing
-------
- Overnight soak test: leave an iPad on /display for 72h,
  assert zero crashes, stable memory, continuous QR rotation.
- Power-cycle test: pull power for 60s, verify auto-return.
- Network-loss test: disable Wi-Fi for 10 min, re-enable,
  verify state converges and QR replaces correctly during the
  gap.
- QR scan reliability: scan from arm's length, iPhone and
  Android, normal corridor lighting, against each state. All
  must succeed within 2s.
- QR token replay: capture, wait 6 min, attempt to use → reject.
- QR token tamper: alter t → reject.
- Composite scan: scan from a COMPOSITE-scoped iPad, land on
  chooser page, book the whole room → succeeds; book just one
  section → succeeds; for a student, the staff-only section is
  disabled.
- Section-scoped iPad: scan, land on the booking dialog for
  that section only — verify there's no path to book the
  composite from this entry point.
- End-to-end timings: scan to confirmed booking <20s when
  signed in, <40s on a fresh MSAL flow.
- Accessibility: screen reader announces status correctly;
  contrast passes in all three states; fallback URL is
  reachable by typing on a phone.


Exit criteria
-------------
- One STANDARD pilot room and one COMPOSITE pilot room have
  mounted iPads running for a full week with no manual
  intervention.
- A signed-in user scanning, signing in if needed, and
  confirming a booking takes <20s on a STANDARD or SECTION iPad,
  <30s on a COMPOSITE iPad (extra step for the chooser).
- A booking made via the API flips the iPad's state within 5s
  in 95% of runs during office hours.
- Device pairing and revocation are documented and demonstrated
  end-to-end for all three scopes.


Risks and mitigations
---------------------
- Safari kills background tabs aggressively. Mitigation: single-
  app mode via MDM ensures we're always foreground.
- iOS doesn't allow permanent prevention of sleep from a web
  app alone. Mitigation: MDM profile sets auto-lock to "Never."
- Older Android phones don't always recognise QR codes from the
  default camera. Mitigation: printed fallback URL on every
  mount; communicate in rollout (phase 8).
- Staff Wi-Fi doesn't reach a basement room → phone can't load
  the portal after scanning. Mitigation: mount checklist
  requires confirming phone signal at install; install an
  additional AP before deploying that iPad.
- A composite-scoped iPad's chooser is the only place
  whole-composite bookings happen from a corridor; if the
  chooser UI is unclear, users will book a section instead.
  Mitigation: usability test with a staff member who hasn't
  seen the UI before, during the composite pilot.


Out of scope for this phase
---------------------------
- Student-facing displays in non-bookable areas.
- Permanent pairing with Teams Rooms hardware.
- A native iOS app — the PWA shell is the deployment surface.
